# Business Crisis Public Data Sources

These public sources can help participants replace the mini dataset with richer materials.
Check each source's license, access terms, and privacy restrictions before using it in a demo.

## SEC Cybersecurity Incidents Database

- URL: https://dukesecurity.ai/incidents
- Use case: Public-company cybersecurity incident examples, including method, affected asset, effect, and business continuity context.
- Good for: Cyber incident response demos and business interruption Q&A.

## Repository of Industrial Security Incidents

- URL: https://www.risidata.com/
- Use case: Industrial control system and SCADA-related cybersecurity incidents.
- Good for: Critical infrastructure or operations-risk demos.

## Institute for Crisis Management Crisis Categories

- URL: https://crisisconsultant.com/crisis-toolbox/
- Use case: Crisis category taxonomy such as recalls, cyber crime, labor disputes, mismanagement, environmental damage, and workplace violence.
- Good for: Building a classification checklist or crisis triage assistant.

## CrisisNet Corporate Default Risk Dataset

- URL: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Sashank-810/crisisnet-dataset
- Use case: Public-company risk and default-oriented Q&A records.
- Good for: Financial distress or risk-signal demos.

## OpenFEMA Data

- URL: https://www.fema.gov/openfema-data-page
- Use case: Disaster, assistance, public infrastructure, and mitigation datasets.
- Good for: Business continuity planning around external disasters.
