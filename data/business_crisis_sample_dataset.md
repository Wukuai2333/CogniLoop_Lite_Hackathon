# Business Crisis Mini Dataset

This is a small synthetic sample dataset for testing CogniLoop Lite's local Cognee assistant.
It is not a complete real-world dataset and should not be treated as legal, financial, or security advice.

The schema is inspired by public crisis-management categories and cyber incident datasets, including:

- Institute for Crisis Management crisis categories
- SEC-disclosed cybersecurity incident indexes
- industrial security incident repositories

## Scenario 1: Regional Retailer Payment Outage

- Organization type: Mid-size regional retailer
- Crisis type: Business interruption, customer service disruption
- Trigger: Payment processor outage during a seasonal sales event
- Immediate impact: Stores can only accept cash for several hours
- Stakeholders: Customers, store managers, payment vendor, finance team
- First response priority: Confirm scope, publish temporary payment guidance, prepare refund policy
- Recommended communication tone: Direct, practical, low-drama
- Follow-up metric: Time to restore card payments and number of unresolved customer complaints

## Scenario 2: Health Software Vendor Data Exposure

- Organization type: B2B health software vendor
- Crisis type: Data exposure, privacy concern
- Trigger: Misconfigured cloud storage bucket exposes non-clinical customer metadata
- Immediate impact: Customers demand incident timeline and remediation plan
- Stakeholders: Customer security teams, legal, compliance, support, executive team
- First response priority: Preserve evidence, close exposure, notify affected customers with verified facts
- Recommended communication tone: Accountable, precise, non-speculative
- Follow-up metric: Time to containment, customer notification completion, audit remediation status

## Scenario 3: Food Manufacturer Recall

- Organization type: Packaged food manufacturer
- Crisis type: Product recall, consumer safety
- Trigger: Supplier quality issue creates possible allergen mislabeling
- Immediate impact: Retail partners pause distribution of affected SKUs
- Stakeholders: Consumers, retailers, supplier, regulators, quality assurance team
- First response priority: Identify affected lots, publish recall instructions, coordinate retailer pullback
- Recommended communication tone: Safety-first, specific, action-oriented
- Follow-up metric: Percentage of affected lots removed and number of consumer inquiries resolved

## Scenario 4: Logistics Provider Ransomware Disruption

- Organization type: Regional logistics provider
- Crisis type: Cyber incident, operations disruption
- Trigger: Ransomware affects dispatch and shipment tracking systems
- Immediate impact: Delivery ETA visibility is reduced and manual dispatch is required
- Stakeholders: Customers, drivers, operations, insurer, incident response vendor
- First response priority: Isolate affected systems, switch to manual continuity plan, update customer ETA policy
- Recommended communication tone: Calm, operational, transparent about known service limits
- Follow-up metric: Shipment backlog, system restoration milestones, customer escalation volume
